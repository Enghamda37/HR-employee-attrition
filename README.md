# HR Analytics: Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes employee data to understand workforce attrition and identify patterns associated with employees leaving an organization.

## Objectives
- Measure the overall employee attrition rate.
- Compare attrition rates across departments.
- Analyze monthly income in relation to attrition.
- Investigate the relationship between overtime and employee attrition.
- Develop data-driven recommendations for HR teams.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Key Areas of Analysis
1. Data cleaning and quality checks
2. Employee attrition distribution
3. Attrition rate by department
4. Monthly income analysis
5. Overtime analysis
6. Business insights and recommendations

## Business Recommendations
- Investigate departments with comparatively high attrition rates.
- Review compensation and career development opportunities.
- Evaluate overtime practices and employee workload.
- Monitor job satisfaction and employee feedback.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how data analysis can help HR teams understand employee attrition patterns and support evidence-based workforce planning.

**Note:** Findings describe patterns and associations in the dataset; they do not establish causation.
